About Us

At the Intelligent Communications and Advanced Computing Lab, we leverage cutting-edge algorithms and innovative machine learning models to tackle complex challenges in the fields of intelligent communications, advanced computing, and information security. Our mission is to create impactful solutions that push the boundaries of modern technology and enhance the efficiency, security, and intelligence of next-generation communication systems.

Our research group is committed to advancing these fields through collaboration, innovation, and rigorous experimentation. By focusing on real-world applications, we aim to address some of the most pressing technological challenges faced by industries today.

Whether it's optimizing wireless communications, developing secure computing systems, or innovating at the intersection of artificial intelligence and communications, we are dedicated to making a meaningful impact.

Research Interests

Her current research interests include machine learning for intelligent wireless communications, non-orthogonal multiple access (NOMA), reconfigurable intelligent surface (RIS), multi-access edge computing (MEC), Semantic Communications and Edge AI, etc. Dr. Fang has been serving as a general chair for EAI GameNets 2022, a publications chair for IEEE VTC 2023 Fall, and a symposium chair for IEEE Globecom 2023, IEEE VTC 2024 Spring and IEEE ICC 2025. Dr. Fang currently serves as an Editor for IEEE Communication Letters and IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society (OJ-COM). She received the Exemplary Reviewer Certificates from the IEEE Transactions on Communications in 2017 and 2021, as well as the Exemplary Editor Certificates from IEEE OJ-COM in 2021 and 2023. Dr. Fang won the IEEE SPCC Early Achievement Award in 2023.

Current Research Topics

Semantic Communications

Semantic Communications revolutionizes how information is transmitted and understood by focusing on the meaning and context behind the data. By enhancing communication systems with semantic awareness, this research enables groundbreaking advancements in network efficiency, significantly reducing bandwidth usage and improving the quality of real-time decision-making in applications such as autonomous systems and smart cities.

Federated Learning

Federated Learning addresses critical privacy and data-sharing concerns by enabling machine learning models to be trained directly on decentralized devices without transferring raw data. This transformative approach not only safeguards user privacy but also unlocks the potential for scalable AI in healthcare, finance, and industrial IoT, driving innovation across domains while minimizing communication overhead.

Intelligent Mobile Edge Computing

Intelligent Mobile Edge Computing brings real-time data processing and AI-driven decision-making closer to the source of data generation, reducing latency and enhancing system responsiveness. This research empowers applications like autonomous vehicles, remote healthcare, and smart factories, delivering unparalleled performance and reliability in dynamic, real-world environments while optimizing network and computational resources.
